# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AstraOS a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for learners following the guide

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Report issues to the project owner (see the GitHub profile associated with this
repository). The owner will review and respond as appropriate, including
warnings or exclusion from project spaces.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
